About Borrower Management System

Borrower Management System

Version 1.0

This system was developed for Maharashtra Lokadhikar Samiti to manage borrower information, debt correspondence, and loan documents.

Features:
  • Maintain comprehensive borrower information
  • Track correspondence with Marathi language support
  • Manage loan-related documents
  • Advanced search functionality
  • Import/Export data with Excel
